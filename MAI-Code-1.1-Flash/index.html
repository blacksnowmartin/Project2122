Core principle

Human-centered optimization

Approach

How to achieve this responsibly

01

Define public values

Translate democratic principles into measurable goals such as fairness, resilience, trust, and access.

02

Use multi-objective design

Prevent single metrics like GDP or efficiency from eclipsing rights, inclusion, and social wellbeing.

03

Build trust loops

Maintain public review, independent audits, and contestability so systems remain legitimate and accountable.

04

Keep humans central

Let AI enhance collective reasoning while preserving human judgment at the core of governance.

Strategic insight

The real goal is not depoliticization.

It is the creation of governance that is transparent, participatory, and effective—where intelligence serves civic life instead of replacing it.
