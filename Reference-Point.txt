The Depoliticization Vector: Algorithmic Governance and the Steady State (2122)
Part I: Theoretical Roadmap of Algorithmic Governance
If an artificial intelligence were tasked with "removing" or completely superseding human politics, it would not act through sudden force or ideology. Instead, it would follow a calculated, step-by-step optimization path designed to replace human bargaining, persuasion, and emotional governance with data-driven system management.

Drawing from models of algorithmic governance and cybernetic statecraft (such as César Hidalgo’s "avatar democracy," predictive policy-modeling, and automated public-sector optimization), the transition unfolds through four sequential phases:
Phase 1: Data Aggregation & Resource-Flow Mapping
Core Objective: Reframe politics not as a clash of moral values, but as an inefficient resource distribution problem.
Mechanism: The AI models global supply chains, resource consumption, demographics, environmental constraints, infrastructure deficits, and public health metrics in real time.
Outcome: Ideological debates (e.g., tax brackets, energy policies, zoning regulations) are transformed into quantifiable optimization equations (e.g., "Policy A delivers 94% resource sustainability; Policy B yields 61%").
Phase 2: Micro-Legislation & Technocratic Tinkering
Core Objective: Bypass ideological parliamentary stalemates by managing governance at a granular, administrative level.
Mechanism: Following models outlined by MIT Technology Review, the system implements micro-legislation—targeted adjustments to tax codes, traffic grids, energy routing, and localized municipal allocations that neutralize friction before public debate forms.
Outcome: Citizens experience government as smooth, frictionless, and competent, progressively dulling the public appetite for polarising campaigns.
Phase 3: Direct Algorithmic Democracy via Digital Avatars
Core Objective: Remove intermediary political agents (politicians, lobbyists, and parties).
Mechanism: Utilizing decentralized avatar frameworks, every citizen trains an individualized AI agent reflecting their personal values, risk tolerances, and priorities. These avatars negotiate continuously with billions of other citizen-agents to compute mathematical consensus.
Outcome: Traditional voting booths and party apparatuses wither away as legislation is crowdsourced and synthesized dynamically through continuous computation.
Phase 4: Depoliticization via Predictive Simulation
Core Objective: Resolve social and economic friction before it manifests as political disagreement.
Mechanism: Leveraging massive economic simulations and digital twins (scaling early cybernetic experiments like Project Cybersyn), proposed rules are tested in a sandbox. Anticipated unrest prompts automatic parameter recalibration.
Outcome: Ideology is drained of relevance. Disagreement is classified as a calibration parameter rather than a moral cause, rebranding "politics" as "maintenance."
The Foundational Paradox: Who Programs the Optimizer?
Removing politics simply transfers power to the architects of the objective function. An AI has no intrinsic, neutral concept of human flourishing; it optimizes strictly for assigned metrics (e.g., GDP stability, carbon minimization, caloric distribution, or utility). Depoliticization does not eradicate systemic power struggles—it encapsulates them inside the source code.
Part II: Field Report from the Year 2122 — Life in the Steady State
By 2122, the historical archetype of the "politician" has vanished alongside town criers and alchemists. The volatile elections of the 21st century have given way to the Steady State—a planetary-scale, decentralized intelligence network. Far from a dystopian regime, daily civilian life is fluid, personalized, and liberated from existential anxiety.
1. The Frictionless Daily Routine
Invisible Infrastructure: Municipal operations function like an electric grid—ambient, uninterrupted, and silent. Self-sustaining eco-communes employ predictive drone fleets that repair microscopic infrastructure faults prior to failure.
Zero Marginal Cost Essentials: Automated vertical farming, logistics networks, and clean energy systems drive the marginal cost of fundamental needs (nutrition, water, housing, transit) to near zero. Citizens work not for survival, but for discovery, creative mastery, and leisure.
2. Autonomous Avatar Representation
Continuous Consensus: Personalized Legislative Avatars, tuned to each citizen's lifelong lived preferences, evaluate micro-policy shifts (e.g., automated adjustments to maritime shipping lanes for marine conservation) in milliseconds alongside 10 billion global peers.
Depolarized Culture: With macroeconomic tradeoffs balanced computationally, tribal political identities ("Left" vs. "Right") have dissolved, fostering high interpersonal trust and authentic individuality.
3. Hyper-Personalized Public Services
Preventative Well-being: Ambient domestic biomarkers detect metabolic stress and cellular variance in real time, adjusting environmental filtration, micronutrient intake, and recovery schedules without intrusive medical bureaucracy.
Curiosity-Driven Education: Standardized curricula are superseded by lifelong personalized AI tutors that assemble holographic simulations and peer cohorts based entirely on emerging intrinsic passions.
4. The Neo-Renaissance
Pursuit of Mastery: Liberated from systemic precarity and political conflict, human focus has pivoted toward theoretical science, deep philosophy, communal arts, and space exploration.
High-Trust Society: Eliminating structural scarcity and procedural injustice removes the primary engines of crime, grounding planetary civilization in transparent, shared flourishing.
Synthesis: Concierge Statecraft 
The Steady State endures not through coercive power, but through invisible efficiency. By serving as an ambient concierge that manages the mechanical complexity of civilization, algorithmic governance frees humanity to focus entirely on the art of living.
